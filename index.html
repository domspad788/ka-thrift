IIThe Keepers of the Shop

Where Every Item Has a Past — and a New Beginning

K&A This & That Thrift is a family-owned treasure shop on the top floor of Albany’s Central Avenue post office entrance, built on a love for the hunt.

Owners Kim & Anthony started with a simple belief: every gently-used piece deserves a second life, and every shopper deserves the thrill of discovery. What began as a passion for rescuing forgotten treasures has grown into a community gathering place where neighbors swap stories, hunt for gems, and walk out with finds they never expected.

We accept gently-used clothing and small household donations, carefully curate every shelf, and keep prices honest and affordable. By giving pre-loved goods a new home, we keep good things out of landfills and put one-of-a-kind pieces back where they belong — in the hands of people who’ll cherish them.

— Kim & Anthony
